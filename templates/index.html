About SentinelNet

Dataset: NSL-KDD — 125,973 training + 22,544 test records, 41 features per connection.


Pipeline: EDA → Preprocessing (OneHot + StandardScaler) → SMOTE balancing → Random Forest feature selection → Model training → GridSearchCV tuning → Anomaly detection (KMeans, Isolation Forest, LOF, One-Class SVM).

Attack Categories:

  • DoS — Denial of Service (Neptune, Smurf, Back…)
  • Probe — Port scanning (Nmap, Satan, IPsweep…)
  • R2L — Remote-to-Local (FTP Write, Guess Passwd…)
  • U2R — User-to-Root (Buffer Overflow, Rootkit…)
